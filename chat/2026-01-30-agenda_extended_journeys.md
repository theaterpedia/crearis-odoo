# AGENDA EXTENDED: JOURNEYS

## Karo -> first journey: customer-onboarding
the process towards entering the A-module 

(example is dated towards: FEB 5th -> INFO-Teaser A0 ID:1598 sharepoint)

Karo (Augsburg -> will travel to München by train) found dasei on the internet as she was looking around, researching for other options because she is not quite happy on the bachelor-program 'Sozialpädagogik' that she runs 2 years already. She is an optimistic and very friendly young woman, 22 years old, easy to talk with on the phone and immediately agrees on using the German 'Du' rather than 'Siezen'. She played theatre earlier at school and now decided to join the 'INFO-Teaser' (a free-to-go 2hours videocall-experience with Hans from dasei).
Along with the info-teaser she wants to understand whether going with courses at dasei would nicely complement her bachelor or whether it is too big (apparently she thinks it is too big, says she is just "researching options" and that one should come after the other).
She is used to do everything with her smartphone and decides rather spontaneously and has understood that dasei serves such a possibility to quickly decide and then maybe to cancel again. She has learned that she should assure this option right at the beginning because she got tricked already twice into long-running contracts by phone-providers and alike where she did not cross-check because of her optimistic attitude.

## Ida -> second journey: becoming a participant
starts with consulting in the middle of A-module

(example is dated towards: OCT 5th)

Ida (Nürnberg, 37 years) already knows dasei for some time now. She is more of the 'practical type', does not like the whole digital world very much. 3 years ago a friend of her had successfully participated in the courses. She had visited one of the shows that time, not thinking that she would ever join this program herself., because she thought it would be more the extroverted type of people that fit here. 
But since then life changed a lot and she found she should try some 'courageous' step. She tried it and after joining the A0 "Basistag" she now participated the A1/A2 + A4/A5 in the 'Block' variant on Burgstallmühle. She now fluently acts on MS Teams Team M18 and is 4 days from the 'consulting' call with Hans. This call is about whether she wants to go on. She rather would, but still thinks this whole thing is rather for other people, even if she had fun especially on A4/A5. She has taken a protocol-task from the A4/A5-weekend. For call with Hans she is well prepared:
- completely has read-through the 50-pages printed curriculum
- looked through the default B,C,D-modules planning for the Block-Seminar-Variant and has 2 issues with events in Early 2027 (during the consulting-call Hans will help her resolve them by choosing alternative events either in München or 1 year later in Nürnberg)
- she has an issue 'hanging' with the invoicing -> the invoice should be split in 2 parts, 400 EUR 'Zuschuss vom Arbeitgeber'

## Jolanda -> third journey: transformation
D-module

(example is dated towards: MAR 5th -> in the midst of D1 = series of online-session + preparing for the D2-Event which takes place at Burgstallmühle)

Jolanda (Bad Tölz, 35 years) is a teacher on secondary school (Realschule). She is much into arts and literature and has been 6 years at university, loves the academic world and is well organised with her notebook. Originally she did not really consider going on at dasei, but there was a minor stunning moment that made here change her mind. Kicked off via a simple well-timed confirmation email she was reminded that she had her decision left unclear. She was astonished to find a personal note as 'P.S.:' added to the bottom of the Email citing a small personal talk about whether she would stay in Bad Tölz or not + hinting her to take contact to a second person from Bad Tölz that joined the program. Ever since then she was tracking that the folks at dasei really to look into the personal questions that form the background of course-participation.
That had caught her interest and she started to join this kind of interactions, found that other participants of her course would be very open to share small but important bits of information. So it was already 3 times that she could sleep over in München instead of heaving to train-travel every morning and evening. Now it is like 'finding friends' there. Communication via the dasei-app (agenda it is called) stays 'on course', doesn't drift into private things.
No this already has run for 1.5 half years, the 'Agenda-App' is like fork and spoon for her. The course entered the D-Module. Jolanda has started to instruct the 'Theater-AG' at her school, in the midst of July they will run a bigger performance with a fully self-created performance based on creative writing and audio-recordings on the streets ob 'Bad Tölz'. It is the 'Wasser-Projekt', they want to perform at the shore and in the Waters of the 'Isar'.
And as Jolanda prepares and asks herself how to bring the infos about that project on the internet she starts to understand that 'agenda' is much more, why this has this 'crearis' name that always had caught her attention: She understands that she is allowed to create her own 'domaincode' their, that it is like setting up her own website. She is not free to do it right away, but she sees a tutorial-link and the greyed button and that she could ask the 'admin' (which is Hans either way) to grant her the privilege - very exciting!

## fourth journey: issue-driver

I don't give a name here, only list the common edge-cases we encounter:
- has a cluttered email-box and several social-media accounts
- takes a lot of consultation
- repeatadly needs to be reconfirmed even if plans do not change at all and still confuses the communication and information (both with instructors and participants)
- uses a screenshotted, outdated pdf-file as their 'agenda' that lives in the image-gallery of their smartphone
- from time to time is sick or misses an event, needs to be replanned
- gets discounted rates because of 'Härtefall' (would not be able to afford the default rates)
- missed payments for 3 months and is overdue
- does not login to odoo, only to sharepoint/teams -> and here only with the phone for video-conferences
- needs a written confirmation of participation in a special format for the 3rd time already
