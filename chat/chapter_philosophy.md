## Abstract

Crearis is an opinionated software strategy supporting movements in "Soziokultur". It rejects isolation/hierarchical thinking, favors "creators" over "owners", centers on events as origin points, and flows across company boundaries. Agenda is crearis's core tool — a formalized timeline of what happens before, during, and after events.
